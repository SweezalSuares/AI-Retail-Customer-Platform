# EDA Insight Narrative — Retail Customer Intelligence Platform

## 1. Segment Overview
The customer base is dominated by **Occasional** shoppers, followed by Regular,
Loyal, and High_Value customers. High_Value customers make up only
**10.5%** of the base but represent the most profitable segment
per the average monthly spend and CLV figures below.

| Segment | Avg Monthly Spend | Avg Annual Income | Avg Purchase Freq | Avg Return Rate | Avg CLV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Occasional | 372.51 | 42277.77 | 3.29 | 0.15 | 11104.15 |
| Regular | 268.97 | 41986.67 | 4.75 | 0.15 | 8763.32 |
| Loyal | 244.83 | 42326.53 | 6.53 | 0.15 | 11824.03 |
| High_Value | 447.22 | 42241.72 | 7.94 | 0.15 | 24277.95 |
